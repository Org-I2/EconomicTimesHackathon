Title: Pressure Vessel Safety Certificate for Storage Tank TK-3050

1. CERTIFICATE DETAILS
This certifies that storage tank TK-3050, located in the chemical storage area, Unit 5,
has been inspected and meets applicable pressure vessel safety regulations under the
Factory Act and OISD guidelines for atmospheric storage tanks.

2. INSPECTION SUMMARY
Tank shell thickness, weld integrity, and relief valve function were tested and found
satisfactory. No corrosion beyond acceptable limits was observed on the tank exterior or
accessible interior surfaces during the inspection.

3. VALIDITY
This certificate is valid for 24 months from the date of issue.
