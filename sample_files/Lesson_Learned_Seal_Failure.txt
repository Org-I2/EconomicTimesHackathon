LESSON LEARNED
Title: Recurring seal filter neglect leading to vibration trips on P-204
Equipment Tag: P-204
Document Type: Lesson Learned

PROBLEM
Pump P-204 experienced repeated high-vibration trips over a 14-month period. Root cause
investigation traced the pattern to inconsistent seal flush filter replacement, which was
supposed to occur every 3 months per SOP-PUMP-204 but was frequently skipped or delayed
during high workload periods, allowing fouling to build up and destabilize the seal flush
pressure, which in turn increased bearing and seal vibration over time.

RESOLUTION
Seal flush filter replacement was added as a mandatory checklist item tied to the monthly
lubrication check (rather than a separate 3-month task that was easy to miss), and a
vibration trend alert was configured to notify reliability engineering automatically when
readings exceed 4.0 mm/s RMS, ahead of the 4.5 mm/s RMS escalation threshold.
