INCIDENT REPORT
Report ID: INC-2026-07
Equipment Tag: P-204
Document Type: Incident Report
Date: 2026-06-30

1. INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
Pump P-204 tripped on high vibration during startup at 06:42 on 2026-06-30. Operator
attempted a single restart per procedure, which also tripped within 90 seconds on the same
high vibration interlock.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS TAKEN
2.1 Pump P-204 was isolated and tagged out pending investigation.
2.2 Standby pump was brought online to maintain process water transfer without interruption.
2.3 Maintenance team was notified and responded within 20 minutes.

3. INITIAL OBSERVATIONS
3.1 Vibration spike at trip was recorded at 9.2 mm/s RMS, well above the 4.5 mm/s RMS
escalation threshold noted in prior inspection reports for this equipment.
3.2 Seal flush filter had not been replaced since the last inspection in November 2024,
exceeding the 3-month replacement interval defined in SOP-PUMP-204.
3.3 Visual inspection found no obvious external leakage or damage at time of trip.

4. CROSS-REFERENCE
This is the third vibration-related trip event logged against P-204 in the past 14 months.
Prior inspection report INS-2024-118 flagged a rising vibration trend on this equipment and
recommended escalation if readings exceeded 4.5 mm/s RMS, which this event confirms.

5. REPORTED BY
Reported by Vinod Sharma, Shift Supervisor.
