Title: Operating and Maintenance Procedure for Centrifugal Pump P-204

1. PURPOSE
This procedure describes the standard operating and preventive maintenance steps for
centrifugal pump P-204, located in the process water transfer skid, Unit 3.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all shift engineers and maintenance technicians responsible for the operation
and upkeep of pump P-204 and its associated piping and instrumentation.

3. OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS
3.1 Before startup, verify suction valve V-101 is fully open and discharge valve is closed.
3.2 Confirm seal flush line pressure is between 2.5 and 3.5 bar before energizing the motor.
3.3 Start the pump and gradually open the discharge valve over 30 seconds to avoid surge.
3.4 Monitor vibration readings; normal operating range is 2.0 to 4.5 mm/s RMS.

4. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE
4.1 Pump P-204 requires a full preventive maintenance inspection every 6 months.
4.2 Seal flush filter should be replaced every 3 months or on high differential pressure alarm.
4.3 Bearing lubrication is to be checked monthly and topped up per the OEM manual.

5. SAFETY NOTES
5.1 Lock out and tag out the motor breaker before any maintenance work.
5.2 Wear hearing protection when working near the pump during operation.
