INSPECTION REPORT
Report ID: INS-2024-118
Equipment Tag: P-204
Document Type: Inspection Report
Date: 2024-11-02

1. SUMMARY
Routine 6-month inspection of centrifugal pump P-204 was carried out as part of the
scheduled preventive maintenance program defined in SOP-PUMP-204.

2. FINDINGS
2.1 Visible corrosion was noted on the pump housing near flange B. Corrosion depth was
estimated at less than 1mm and does not currently affect structural integrity.
2.2 Vibration readings measured at 3.8 mm/s RMS, within the normal operating range but
trending upward compared to the previous inspection (2.9 mm/s RMS in May 2024).
2.3 Seal flush filter showed moderate fouling; filter was replaced during this inspection.
2.4 No abnormal noise or leakage was observed during the operational check.

3. RECOMMENDATIONS
3.1 Recommend re-inspection of flange B corrosion at the next scheduled maintenance window.
3.2 Continue monitoring vibration trend; escalate to reliability engineering if readings
exceed 4.5 mm/s RMS before the next scheduled inspection.
